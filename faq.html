Booking & Services

How do I book a service?

Booking is simple! You can:

  • Visit our Book Service page
  • Fill out the booking form with your requirements
  • We'll match you with verified professionals
  • Confirm booking via WhatsApp with service details

The entire process typically takes 1-2 hours for confirmation.

What services do you offer?

We offer four premium services:

  • Home Tutor & Teaching Services: Personalized educational support for all subjects and levels
  • Fitness Trainer Services: Customized workout plans at home or gym
  • Caretaker Services: Professional care for elderly, children, or special needs
  • Physiotherapist Services: Home visit physiotherapy and rehabilitation

How quickly can I get a service?

Service availability varies:

  • Immediate (Same Day): Available within 4-6 hours for urgent requirements
  • Standard Booking: 12-24 hours advance notice recommended
  • Regular/Scheduled: Weekly or monthly schedules can be arranged

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for guaranteed availability.

Can I book services for multiple days or weeks?

Yes! We offer flexible booking options:

  • Single Session: One-time service booking
  • Weekly Packages: 3-5 sessions per week at discounted rates
  • Monthly Packages: Regular scheduled services with best pricing
  • Custom Plans: Tailored to your specific needs and schedule

Contact us via WhatsApp for custom package details and pricing.

Pricing & Payments

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on:

  • Service Type: Each service has different base rates
  • Duration: Per hour, per session, or package rates
  • Professional Level: Certified vs. advanced specialists
  • Location: Service area within the city

Typical range: ₹500 - ₹2000 per session. Exact pricing is shared during WhatsApp confirmation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We offer multiple payment options:

  • Cash: Direct payment to the professional after service
  • UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, PayTM
  • Bank Transfer: NEFT/IMPS to our account
  • Digital Wallets: Various payment apps

Payments are typically made after service completion unless booking a package.

Are there any hidden charges?

No hidden charges! Our pricing is transparent:

  • Service Fee: Clearly mentioned during booking
  • Travel Cost: Included if within city limits (extra for outskirts)
  • Materials/Equipment: Additional if required (will be informed)
  • Taxes: GST as applicable

All costs are communicated upfront before confirmation. No surprises!

Do you offer refunds?

Our refund policy is customer-friendly:

  • Full Refund: If service is cancelled 24 hours before scheduled time
  • Partial Refund: 50% refund for cancellations within 24 hours
  • No Refund: For no-shows or last-minute cancellations
  • Service Issues: Full refund if professional doesn't meet agreed standards

Refunds are processed within 5-7 working days to the original payment method.

Professionals

How do you verify your professionals?

Every professional undergoes rigorous verification:

  • Background Check: Police verification and address proof
  • Certificate Verification: Educational and professional certificates
  • Experience Validation: Previous employment verification
  • Skill Assessment: Practical tests and interviews
  • Reference Checks: Contacting previous clients/employers

Only 30% of applicants pass our verification process.

Can I choose a specific professional?

Yes! We offer three options:

  • Auto-Assign: We match you with the best available professional
  • Professional Selection: Browse profiles and choose your preferred professional
  • Request Specific: If you want to continue with a professional you liked

Professional profiles include photos, qualifications, experience, and client ratings.

What if I'm not satisfied with the professional?

Your satisfaction is guaranteed:

  • Immediate Replacement: Request a different professional at no extra cost
  • Quality Check: Our team will follow up to resolve issues
  • Feedback System: Rate your experience to help us improve
  • Escalation Support: Contact our support team for urgent issues

We maintain a satisfaction rate of 98% across all services.

Safety & Verification

Are your professionals safe and reliable?

Absolutely! Our safety measures include:

  • Complete Background Verification: Police clearance mandatory
  • ID Proof Verification: Aadhar card, PAN card verification
  • Training: Professional ethics and conduct training
  • Real-time Tracking: Service tracking and check-in system
  • Emergency Support: 24/7 helpline for safety concerns

All professionals wear ID badges and carry verification documents.

What COVID-19 safety measures do you follow?

We maintain strict COVID-19 protocols:

  • Vaccination: All professionals fully vaccinated
  • Daily Health Checks: Temperature monitoring and symptom screening
  • Safety Gear: Masks, gloves, and sanitizers provided
  • Sanitization: Regular sanitization of equipment
  • Contactless Service: Options available where possible

You can request additional safety measures during booking.

Is my personal information secure?

Your privacy is our priority:

  • Data Encryption: SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Limited Access: Only essential personnel access your information
  • Professional Confidentiality: Professionals sign confidentiality agreements
  • Data Protection: Compliance with data protection regulations
  • Secure Payments: Payment information is never stored

We never share your personal information with third parties without consent.
