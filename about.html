Our Story

The Journey of Connecting Quality Services With People

Building Trust in Service Industry

Service Mania was founded in 2026 with a simple yet powerful vision: to create a reliable platform where customers can easily connect with verified professionals for their daily service needs.

We recognized the growing need for trustworthy service providers in the domains of education, fitness, healthcare, and home care. Our platform bridges the gap between skilled professionals and customers seeking quality services.
